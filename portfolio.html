Le Cerf Thym

Brand-new website The client www.lecerfthym.fr

The brief: give Le Cerf Thym, a restaurant at the foot of the Sully-sur-Loire castle, a showcase worthy of its table — the menu and daily specials, booking, and a European clientele welcomed in its own language.

Scope
7 pages (home, menu, drinks, surroundings, access, legal, privacy) in 4 languages — French, English, German, Dutch — with a persistent language choice; real Google reviews showcased continuously; photo and video galleries; an interactive territory map (Paris → Orléans → Sully); an interactive access map with Google Maps and Waze routes.
Tech
Static HTML/CSS/JS site, no framework, responsive and lightweight; a custom translation system; a full-screen viewer for the printed menu; HTTPS, compression, caching and security headers; shared hosting.
SEO
Restaurant structured data (address, opening hours, geolocation), Open Graph on every page — a local business's local SEO, done properly.
Compliance
A proper privacy policy, linked from every page: what the site collects, what it does not, and the visitor's rights. A restaurant shouldn't have to discover the subject the day someone asks.
Measurement
A private dashboard, operated by NSY and fed daily from the server logs: real visitors excluding bots, pages read, referrers, and the audience's geographic origin on a world map you can zoom down to city level — with no IP address ever kept. The reach of Facebook and Instagram posts lands in the same place. The restaurant gets figures; it has no tool to administer.

PRV Concept

Redesign of an existing site The lab www.prv-concept.com

The brief: the leading PRV V6 association (Peugeot · Renault · Volvo) already had a site, a forum and a shop — scattered and ageing. The redesign rebuilt the showcase to match the heritage without losing anything: phpBB forum and shop kept and wired back in, historical content carried over, addresses preserved.

Scope
8 sections (history, engines, vehicles, tuners, prototypes, pop culture, library…) + 25 engine / vehicle / prototype entries, all bilingual; a library of digitised period documents readable online; integrated phpBB forum and shop. The FAQ has grown from 87 to 226 questions: the workshop manuals and period brochures in the library were mined one by one, their technical figures turned into sourced answers.
Tech
Static HTML/CSS/JS site, no framework, responsive and lightweight; English pages generated by a custom pipeline — the French content stays the single source, zero drift between languages; built-in PDF reader for the scanned archives; optimised intro video; shared hosting.
Heritage rescued
Twenty years of forum posts displayed their photographs from an outside image host that was shutting down. Over 3,000 images were repatriated onto the association's own server and the posts repointed to them, member signatures included. Without it, two decades of documented tutorials and restorations would have gone blank.
SEO / GEO
A JSON-LD entity graph distributed down to the individual entries, site + forum sitemaps, hreflang and canonicals; a rich FAQ and structured content built for Google as much as for generative engines (GEO / LLMO). The forum sitemap now declares each topic's canonical URL — a sitemap only covers its own directory, and the forum's was submitting none.
AI assistant
“Père Hervé”, a conversational chatbot with an animated mascot, grounded on the site's data and wired live into the shop and the forum — it answers about products as well as community topics. Resilient fallback (real data is still served if the model is down) and strictly factual, never pointing off-site. Forum life is automated as well: registration validation is handled by a script running in a task scheduler, and the mascot — Père Hervé himself — sends the welcome private messages and replies to new members' introduction posts. Since September 2026 he also watches over the forum: he drafts replies to topics left unanswered and to threads where he is mentioned, queued for human validation before anything is posted — with no workshop source or forum thread to rely on he writes nothing, and any claim no source supports is removed and rephrased. Nothing goes out without a human click.
Journal & social
Six in-depth bilingual articles, with RSS feeds, view and “like” counters, and an automated publishing chain: an article goes out to the Facebook Page and to Instagram in one command, as video, with the back-links placed in the first comment — and every article links back to its posts. Each article also opens its own forum topic: readers move from reading to discussing in one click, and the community carries the article on where it already lives.
Compliance
Legal notice and privacy policy written and published in French and in English — an association running a forum, a shop and a membership list cannot do without them.
Measurement
A private dashboard fed daily from the server logs: traffic split across showcase, forum and shop, AI crawler reads, visits that genuinely came from an assistant's answer, chatbot activity, YouTube channel audience, and the reach of Facebook and Instagram posts — down to each individual publication. A conversational agent answers questions about the period on screen, the page exports to PDF or image, and the raw logs are archived daily so tomorrow's unforeseen question can still be asked of yesterday's data. Added since: Search Console collected automatically every day (clicks, impressions, average position, the queries that bring people in), and visitors' geographic origin on a world map you can zoom down to city level — with no IP address ever kept.
